# Design Gap & Defect Register — Freight Reverse-Auction Marketplace

Consolidated from D1-D6 (design-phase agents) plus two findings made at assembly. **Ranked by
consequence if shipped as-is**, most severe first. Two rows at the bottom are already **CLOSED** — kept
in the register so they are not rediscovered and re-worked. One row is a **SOURCE CONFLICT** — a
contradiction between the BRD and the FRD, not a design question; it is not resolved here.

Nothing in this register was resolved, decided, or scored by the assembler. Every `[NEEDS INPUT]` and
every build-blocking decision stays open — see `DESIGN-STRUCTURE.md` §4 for those three, stated with
options only.
