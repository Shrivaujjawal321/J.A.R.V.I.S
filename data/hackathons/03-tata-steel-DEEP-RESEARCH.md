### Tata Steel AI Hackathon 2026 — THE relevant one
- **First-time format in 2026** (no past winner blogs to study)
- **3-round hiring funnel** disguised as hackathon
- 1,400+ registrations already
- Round 1 = leaderboard ML, Round 2 = agentic AI, Final = business interview

### Winning patterns (inferred from Tata Steel's deployed AI + jury composition):
1. **Industrial domain specificity** beats generic ML — name the plant problem (Hot Strip Mill defects, silicon prediction) not "I applied XGBoost"
2. **Two-model architectures** mirror their real stack (anomaly detector → multi-class classifier, per Xomnia case study)
3. **Agentic AI explicitly the R2 priority** — LLM agent with tool use + sensor queries + maintenance tickets wins over pure ML
4. **Business framing is non-negotiable in final** — judges are VPs of Supply Chain / TQM / Manufacturing. Lead with ROI slide, not architecture diagram.
5. **Sustainability + safety framing scores higher** than pure efficiency — energy-cut narrative beats throughput-boost narrative

## 5. Three Options (Boss Decides)

### Option A: Full commit
- **Time:** 80-100 hours, May 12 → Jun 15
- **Outcome:** R1 top 25-35% with prep (advancing possible but not likely without domain reps); R2 genuinely competitive if advancing
- **Risk:** McpIndex deprioritized, resume stays broken, R1 elimination = zero return on 80-100 hours
- **Hidden upside:** Only hackathon in radar with direct hiring outcome. Forces ML pipeline template build (reusable for future comps).

### Option B: Lightweight solo entry
- **Time:** 20-30 hours
- **Outcome:** Likely R1 elimination, no portfolio artifact
- **Verdict:** Worst-of-both-worlds. Not recommended.

### Option C: Skip + redirect bandwidth
- **Time:** Zero on Tata Steel. Redirect to: resume overhaul (P0), McpIndex DevNetwork final polish, Google Cloud Rapid Agent Hackathon (Jun 11, $50K, agentic, no ML gate)
- **Outcome:** McpIndex submits clean, resume starts generating interviews, alternative agentic hackathon with better skill fit
- **Risk:** Misses the only PPI/direct-job hackathon in current radar
- **Hidden upside:** Google Cloud Rapid Agent Hackathon is better skill fit; placing there signals to product/GenAI companies Boss actually wants
