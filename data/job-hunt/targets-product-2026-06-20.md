## Channel Caveats (Critical Reading)

### Cold Email = Near-Zero ROI for These Companies
For Swiggy, Meesho, PhonePe, Zomato, Razorpay, Sprinklr, CRED, Glance/InMobi, Yellow.ai, Fractal, Uniphore, Observe.AI — **cold email to careers@ will go to a void**. These companies receive thousands of applications. The ONLY effective channels are:

1. **ATS portal apply** (required baseline — every application must go through their Greenhouse/Workday/Lever)
2. **LinkedIn referral or recruiter DM** — find either (a) a recruiter at the company, or (b) an engineer who can refer you. Referral bypasses the 95% resume black-hole rate.
3. **Hackathon contact** — Tata Steel Hackathon R2 creates network. Any judge, mentor, or co-participant working at these companies is a warm referral source.

### Where Cold Email / DM Can Work
- **Locus.sh** (careers@locus.sh — small team, ~150 people)
- **Wingify/VWO** (small Delhi startup, founder-accessible)
- **Hasura** (small, engineer-led culture)
- **Dukaan** (startup, DM to founder/CTO on Twitter or LinkedIn)
- **Postman** (mid-size, LinkedIn DM to eng manager feasible)

### LinkedIn Recruiter DM Template Advice
Lead with the ONE specific hook: "I built X (live project) which does Y — I saw you're hiring for Z." Under 5 lines. Include the GitHub/live URL. Do not paste resume.

### Experience Level Realism
Many roles listed here prefer 2-4 years. Ujjawal's competitive advantage is:
- Tata Steel National AI/ML Hackathon **R2 qualifier** (national-level recognition)
- Live production systems (Jarvis, EDITH, McpIndex, EngiNerd) — not toy projects
- Agentic AI depth (93-agent system, RAG, multi-modal) — genuinely rare for a 2025 grad

Position these as equivalent to 1-2 years of industry experience. Acknowledge the profile honestly; don't falsify experience years.
