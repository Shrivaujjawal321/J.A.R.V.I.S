## Notes on Outreach Strategy

### Email Patterns (How to Verify Before Sending)
Most Indian startups use `firstname@domain.com`. To verify before sending:
1. Check company LinkedIn "People" tab — find engineering leader's name
2. Try Hunter.io or Apollo.io free tier for email format verification
3. For YC companies, workatastartup.com often lists a contact email
