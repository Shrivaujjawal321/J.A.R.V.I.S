## Teaching method (har session)

1. **1 chhota real-life topic** (10-15 min) — aaj kya kiya, Jarvis pe kya banaya, etc.
2. Boss **4-5 lines English mein** bolta/likhta hai (Telegram voice best for speaking).
3. Jarvis correction format:
   - ❌ **Aapne kaha:** <galti>
   - ✅ **Natural way:** <theek>
   - 💡 **Kyun:** <ek-line reason>
4. **2-3 ready-to-use phrases** roz (native log jo bolte hain).
5. **Gentle** — galti = lesson, sharminda nahi karna.
6. Common galtiyan + seekhe phrases yahin log karo → repeat-mistake pakdo, revise karwao.

## Mistakes ledger (recurring → drill these)
- **"I" capital** — wrote lowercase "i" 12+ times (Lesson 1). TOP priority drill.
- **Past-tense consistency** — slips to present in past narration: build→built, play→played, hangout→hung out (Lesson 1).
- **Meals = have/had, not take** — "took breakfast/dinner" → "had breakfast/dinner" (Lesson 1).
- **"came to home" → "came home"** — no "to" with home (Lesson 1). (But "went to the office" correct.)
- **"lot's of" → "a lot of"** (Lesson 1).
- **Third-person -s** (NEW, Lesson 2) — "it make me" → "it makes me". he/she/it + verb+s. Worth re-drilling.
- **confident (adj) vs confidence (noun)** — "full of confident" → "full of confidence" (Lesson 2).
- **Singular/plural match** — "this is my strategies" → "these are my strategies"; "all the game" → "all the games" (Lesson 2).
- Spelling watch: freshened, colleague, my friend (spacing), then (not "ten"), strategies, sometimes (one word), continuously.

- **"the best/superlative + the"** (NEW, Lesson 3) — "a best person" → "the best person".
- **"features" → "qualities"** for people (Lesson 3).
- **⚠️ "I" never takes verb+s** (NEW, Lesson 4) — over-generalized the he/she+s rule: wrote "I works" → "I work". Boundary: I/you/we/they = plain verb; he/she/it = +s. KEY clarification.
- **"to" + base verb** (Lesson 4) — "to achiving" → "to achieve". After "to", verb stays plain (no -ing).
- **Over-capitalizing common nouns** (NEW, Lesson 5) — "Intelligent System", "Company", "Agentic System" capitalized. Only proper nouns + sentence-start. Re-drill.
- **Present perfect for recent-completed** (NEW, Lesson 5) — "I build several project in last few months" → "I have built several projects in the last few months". Interview-critical.
- **Plurals for general nouns** — "several project" → "projects"; "intelligent system" → "systems" (Lesson 5).
- **🆕 RUN-ON SENTENCES** (NEW, Lesson 6 — top priority now) — chains many ideas into one long line without full stops. Teach: one complete idea = one sentence + period. Biggest readability gap now that grammar is solid.
- **AI/it + verb+s** (Lesson 6) — "if AI take over", "AI kill", "AI provide" → takes/kills/provides. AI = "it", needs +s.
- **"gonna/wanna" = speech only** (Lesson 6) — in writing use "going to"/"want to". "which gonna hapen" → "which are going to happen".
- **"there is" vs "there are"** (Lesson 6) — plural → "there are two possibilities".

## Phrases learned
- L1: "I hung out with..." (time bitana) · "a lot of work" (never "lot's of").
- L2: "in a row" (lagataar, > "continuously") · "full of confidence".
- L3: "One thing I like the most about ___ is that..." · "He is my idol / role model".
- L4: "To achieve this goal, I'm working on..." (interview-useful) · "live a luxurious life".
- L5: "I have built / I have worked on..." (experience, interview-gold) · "What I love the most is...".
- L6: "At present, we... / Currently, we..." · "There are two possibilities..." (opinion/analysis). Connecting word "because" used correctly ✅.
