A steel plant never really sleeps — and when a critical asset fails without warning, every minute of unplanned downtime burns money and risk.

For Round 2 of the Tata Steel National AI/ML Hackathon, that was the problem I set out to solve.

The problem 👇
In heavy industry, maintenance is still mostly reactive. By the time a pump, motor or furnace throws a fault, the damage is done. The knowledge to act fast — failure histories, P&IDs, SOPs, past root causes — sits buried across manuals and logs an engineer can't search mid-crisis. Slow diagnosis, guesswork on root cause, no reliable read on how long an asset has left.

What I built — EDITH, an agentic maintenance copilot 👇
A 5-agent chain that takes a live alarm end to end: Diagnosis → Root Cause → Remaining-Useful-Life → Prioritization → Recommendations.
• Grounded on a purpose-built 1.25M-row physics-based dataset — every number from structured ML, not an LLM guess.
• A hybrid RAG pipeline with a faithfulness gate that scores every claim against its source — zero hallucinated facts.
• Live monitoring of 15 assets that auto-fires diagnosis on the first sustained alarm. Fast path < 300ms.
• All six required outputs incl. a downloadable PDF report. Qualified into Round 2.
