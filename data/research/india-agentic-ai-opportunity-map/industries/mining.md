# India Agentic AI Opportunity Map — Mining & Minerals

**Vertical deep-dive | Date: 2026-06-23 | Status: Draft research note for review**

> Scope: Indian mining & minerals enterprises ₹100 Cr – ₹1,00,000+ Cr revenue (coal, iron ore, bauxite/aluminium, zinc/lead, limestone, manganese, critical minerals). Focus: agentic AI (autonomous multi-agent systems with human-in-loop), 3–12 month value horizon. Not investment advice.

---

## I. Industry Context (why now)

- **Market size:** Indian metals & mining recorded ~US$285.28 bn revenue in 2024, CAGR ~11.7% (2019–24); sector projected toward US$1.5 trillion by 2035 (Source: IBEF / GlobeNewswire India Metals & Mining Report 2025, Jan 2026, https://www.ibef.org/industry/metals-and-mining ; https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/05/3212969/28124/en/India-Metals-Mining-Industry-Report-2025-Profiles-of-Vedanta-Coal-India-Hindalco-Industries-Tata-Steel.html).
- **GDP push:** Mining ~1.8–2% of GDP today; govt target to **double to ~5% by 2030** (Source: IBEF / Indian Infrastructure, Apr 2026, https://indianinfrastructure.com/2026/04/08/expanding-operations-key-trends-driving-mining-sector-growth/).
- **Production scale:** India is #4 iron-ore producer; iron-ore output projected ~318.8 Mt in 2026 (+3.6% YoY) (Source: GlobeNewswire India Iron Ore Report 2026, Apr 2026).
- **Major players:** Coal India Ltd (CIL) + subsidiaries, NMDC, Vedanta, Hindustan Zinc, Hindalco, Tata Steel mining div, SCCL, NLC India, JSW, Adani Natural Resources.
- **Critical minerals tailwind:** National Critical Mineral Mission — **₹34,000 Cr sovereign fund**, 1,200 exploration projects target by 2030–31 (Source: PIB / ORF / IEA, 2025).

**Why-now setup:** (1) Production-doubling mandate strains evacuation, safety, and compliance bandwidth simultaneously. (2) DGMS 2025 tightened safety (ADAS mandates) → fresh telemetry data exhaust ready for agents. (3) Critical-mineral exploration surge creates greenfield data-orchestration demand. (4) PSU + private capex on FMC/digitization means budget exists. [analysis]

## II. Structural Pain Points (first-principles)

| Lens | Core pain | India-specific cost driver |
|------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Maintenance | Haul-truck/dragline unplanned failure | ~$20,000/hr lost productivity per truck; one-hour heavy-industry downtime ₹10–50 L (Source: Mining Digital / aigrants.in 2026) |
| Safety/compliance | 60–80 fatal + 150+ serious accidents/yr (2020–24); DGMS enquiry burden | 226 coal/lignite deaths in 5 yrs (Source: parliamentary reply, Mar 2025 / FACTLY) |
| Logistics | Rail evacuation bottleneck; FMC delays 12–18 months | Domestic landed coal cost > imported at distant plants (Source: IEEFA / EY 2025) |
| Regulatory | MMDR + royalty + DMF (≤1/3 royalty) + GST on mining rights + state-wise reporting | Multi-statute, multi-state filing maze (Source: MMDR Act amended 27.01.2026; SBS & Co GST analysis) |
| Revenue leakage | Illegal mining + grade/weighbridge fraud | Up to ₹50,000 Cr revenue loss cited from one ban episode (Source: Wikipedia Mining scams; Shankar IAS) |
| Exploration | Greenfield critical-mineral data orchestration | ₹34,000 Cr mission, fragmented GSI/MECL/state data (Source: PIB) |
| Procurement | Capital-spares + diesel + explosives spend | Diesel + ANFO are largest variable opex in opencast [estimate] |
| ESG/decarbon | BRSR + emissions + DMF community spend reporting | Scope 1/2/3 disclosure rising under SEBI BRSR |

## III. The 12 Agentic AI Opportunities

Each opportunity below is detailed in the structured object accompanying this brief. Summary ranking (composite of pain × feasibility × market):

1. **Predictive Maintenance & Fleet-Health Orchestration Agent** — autonomous HEMM uptime.
2. **Mine-Safety Sentinel & DGMS Compliance Agent** — incident prevention + auto-enquiry drafting.
3. **Coal/Ore Evacuation & Logistics Orchestration Agent** — rake/road dispatch optimization.
4. **Statutory & Royalty Compliance Co-Pilot** — MMDR/DMF/GST/state filings.
5. **Grade-Control & Ore-Blending Intelligence Agent** — pit-to-plant quality + value recovery.
6. **Revenue-Leakage & Illegal-Mining Detection Agent** — satellite + weighbridge + GPS fusion.
7. **Procurement & Spares Intelligence Agent** — diesel/explosives/spares spend.
8. **Blast Design & Drill-Fragmentation Optimization Agent** — explosive efficiency.
9. **Exploration & Critical-Mineral Data Orchestration Agent** — NCMM greenfield targeting.
10. **ESG / BRSR / Carbon Reporting Agent** — disclosure + DMF community spend.
11. **Energy & Diesel/Power Optimization Agent** — variable opex reduction.
12. **Executive Mine-Performance Decision-Support Agent** — multi-mine command center.

## IV. Competitive Landscape & Gap

- **Incumbents:** Hexagon Mining (pit-to-port suite, fleet mgmt, slope monitoring), Cyient (India-based; advanced process control, AI orchestration, predictive analytics), IBM, SAP (ERP/EAM), Wenco, Modular Mining (Komatsu), Maptek/Datamine (geology), drone-survey players.
- **The gap:** These are **point tools and dashboards**, not autonomous multi-agent operators. They surface insights; a human still triages, decides, drafts the filing, books the rake, raises the work-order. India-specific statutory drafting (DGMS enquiry, MMDR/DMF/GST), Hinglish field-ops, and PSU procurement integration are largely unserved. Agentic layer that *closes the loop* (decide → draft → integrate → human-approve → execute) is the white space. [analysis]
- Asia-Pacific = 42.9% of AI-in-mining market 2025 (Source: MarketsandMarkets, 2025).

---

## V. Counter-View (steel-manned)

PSU-dominated buyers (CIL controls ~majority of coal) have **long, conservative procurement cycles**, strong unions wary of automation-led headcount fear, and patchy OT/IT data maturity at pit level. Connectivity in remote mines is poor; sensor retrofits are capex-heavy. Agentic autonomy in a **safety-critical, regulated** environment will face "human-must-decide" resistance and liability ambiguity. Realistic near-term wins are **decision-support + drafting agents** (low blast radius) rather than fully autonomous control. ROI claims must survive a 12–18 month PSU sales cycle. [analysis]

## VI. Open Questions

1. What is real OT-data availability at Tier-2 private mines vs PSUs? (Determines feasibility of telemetry-heavy agents.)
2. Will DGMS/Ministry of Mines accept AI-drafted statutory submissions with human sign-off, and under what liability frame?
3. Can a vendor crack PSU GeM/tender procurement, or is private-sector (Vedanta/Adani/JSW/Tata) the faster wedge?
