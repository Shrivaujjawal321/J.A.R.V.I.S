**The gap:** Almost everything in India is a **dashboard, classifieds engine, or vertical CRM**. None are autonomous multi-agent systems that *act* — chase a missed RERA filing, draft a CAM reconciliation, negotiate a renewal, or auto-progress a stalled loan file. Global agentic players (Cactus AI underwriting, Prophia lease abstraction, MagicDoor PM) have no India-tuned analog handling RERA/CERSAI/DILRMP/GST nuances. [analysis]

## V. Counter-View (steel-manned)

The strongest argument against agentic AI in Indian real estate: **the highest-value moments are irreducibly human and offline.** A ₹2 Cr flat closes on a site visit, a relationship, a negotiated discount, and family consensus — not an autonomous agent. The back-office wins (RERA filings, CAM, lease abstraction, loan docs) are real but are *cost-center automation*, not revenue transformation, and the buyers (developers) are notoriously slow tech adopters with fragmented, dirty, paper-based data that breaks every ingestion pipeline. State-level RERA portal fragmentation, non-digitized land records in most states, and the absence of clean lender/registry APIs mean an "autonomous" agent will spend 80% of its effort on data plumbing and human escalation — i.e., it's RPA-with-LLM-glue, not true agency. Add DPDP Act exposure on customer PII and the compliance overhead may exceed the savings for mid-tier firms. The honest read: 3–12 month ROI is real only in **document-heavy, rules-bound, repeatable** workflows; high-judgment leasing/sales remains copilot, not autopilot (consistent with global evidence — Source: AdventuresInCRE, 2026, https://www.adventuresincre.com/ai-tools-commercial-real-estate/).

## VI. Open Questions

1. **Data access:** Can agents get reliable programmatic access to state RERA portals, DILRMP land records, CERSAI, and court databases — or will they be screen-scraping fragile portals? This determines whether half these ideas are "agentic" or "brittle RPA."
2. **Buyer willingness-to-pay:** Will fragmented developers actually pay SaaS-plus-outcome pricing, or do they expect one-time project fees? The full-stack PropTech pivot suggests transaction-take-rate monetization beats SaaS here.
3. **DPDP & liability:** Where does liability sit when an agent files a wrong RERA QPR or a faulty title opinion? Legal indemnity design is the gating risk for compliance/legal agents.
